Visual Attention to Signals in Crowdfunding Decisions

Introduction
You are invited to take part in a research study conducted by Mehrdad Alikhani, Farinoosh Zarinmakan, and Dr. John B. Ford at Old Dominion University. This study aims to understand how individuals pay attention to different types of information (creator and crowd signals) when making funding decisions on crowdfunding platforms.

Description of the Research Study
If you choose to participate, you will complete a short computer-based activity that uses the webcam to estimate your eye movements while viewing four simulated crowdfunding webpages. The session will take approximately 5–10 minutes to complete. The webcam will be used only to estimate gaze position and will not record or store any video.

Exclusion Criteria
You should have completed a pre-screening questionnaire confirming the following:

  • You are 21 years of age or older.
  • You have normal or corrected-to-normal vision (e.g., glasses or contact lenses).
  • You are comfortable using the computer provided for this study, and its built-in webcam will track your eye movements during the task.
  • You should not have any uncorrected visual impairments or medical conditions that could affect concentration or eye movements.

Risks and Benefits
The risks associated with this study are minimal and no greater than those encountered in typical computer use. There are no direct benefits to you for participating; however, your participation will contribute to research on decision-making and attention in online crowdfunding environments.

Confidentiality
The study is anonymous. No identifying information will be collected, and no one will be able to link your responses or gaze data to your identity. Data will be stored securely on a password-protected laboratory computer accessible only to the research team.

Withdrawal Privilege
Participation in this study is voluntary. You may skip any part of the activity or stop at any time without penalty.
